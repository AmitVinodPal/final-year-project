Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal age to start learning chess?
Chess can be learned at any age! We have students from 5 years to 65 years. For children, we recommend starting between 5-7 years when cognitive development is rapid, but we've seen success stories at all ages.
Do I need to own a chess board to start?
Not necessarily for the first few sessions. We provide digital boards for online classes. However, we recommend purchasing a standard tournament board once you decide to continue regular training.
How long does it take to become good at chess?
With consistent practice (3-4 hours weekly), most students show significant improvement within 3-6 months. To reach competitive levels, 1-2 years of dedicated training is typical. Our structured curriculum accelerates this process.
Can chess really help in academic performance?
Absolutely! Studies show chess improves concentration, logical thinking, problem-solving skills, and memory—all crucial for academic success. Many of our students report better grades in mathematics and sciences.
What's the class size for group sessions?
We maintain small batch sizes of 6-8 students for personalized attention. For beginners, we sometimes have slightly larger groups (up to 12) but always with multiple coaches to ensure individual guidance.

Our Story

About Gurukul Chess Academy

Our Journey

Founded in 2003, Gurukul Chess Academy began as a humble initiative by Grandmaster Rohan Sharma with a vision to make chess education accessible to everyone. What started in a small room with just 5 students has now grown into one of the most respected chess academies in the country, training over 1000 students across multiple cities.

Our Philosophy

At Gurukul, we believe chess is more than just a game—it's a tool for cognitive development, character building, and life skills training. Our teaching methodology blends ancient Indian gurukul traditions with modern pedagogical techniques, creating a holistic learning environment.

The Gurukul Difference

Unlike conventional chess coaching centers, we focus on personalized mentorship. Each student gets a customized learning plan based on their strengths, weaknesses, and goals. Our coaches don't just teach moves; they mentor students to think strategically, handle pressure, and learn from both victories and defeats.

Infrastructure & Facilities

Our centers are equipped with tournament-standard chess boards, digital demonstration boards, a comprehensive chess library, and video analysis facilities. For online students, we provide access to our exclusive learning portal with recorded lessons, practice exercises, and interactive tools.

Community Impact

Beyond competitive training, we run community outreach programs introducing chess in underprivileged schools, organize free weekend workshops, and host the annual "Gurukul Open" tournament that attracts players from across the region. We're proud to have made chess a vibrant part of our community's cultural fabric.
